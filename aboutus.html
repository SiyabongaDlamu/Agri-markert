About Agri‑Market Hub

Connecting you directly to local farmers – fresh, fair, and sustainable.

Our Mission

To empower local farmers and provide urban consumers with the freshest, most affordable produce – while building a transparent, sustainable food system.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every farmer gets fair value for their harvest and every family can access healthy, locally‑grown food with a single click.

Our Promise

Zero middlemen, honest pricing, same‑day dispatch from farm to your table. We guarantee freshness and traceability.

Our Story

Agri‑Market Hub was born in 2023 when a group of tech entrepreneurs and small‑scale farmers in Mpumalanga realised that traditional supply chains were failing both producers and consumers. We built a platform that cuts out unnecessary intermediaries, allowing farmers to sell directly and buyers to get the best quality at lower prices.

Today, we proudly work with over 150 local farmers across Limpopo, Mpumalanga, Western Cape, and KZN. Our community has grown to more than 12,000 happy customers, and we're just getting started.
